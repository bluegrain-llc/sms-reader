SMS CODE RELAY · ACCOUNT

Your information stays under your control.

SMS Code Relay lets apps you approve request upcoming SMS codes, information and decisions.

What the service handles

Google or Apple account identity, registered-device and notification identifiers, approved caller connections, request definitions, submitted codes and answers, and delivery/status metadata. Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Tasks and Cloud Run provide the service. The full SMS stays on your phone; it is never uploaded.

Retention and access

SMS codes are retrievable only by the originating connection for two minutes. Form answers are retrievable for 24 hours. SMS request metadata expires seven days after creation, and SMS audit events seven days after each event. Form request definitions, outcomes and metadata events expire seven days after termination. Scheduled cleanup and asynchronous Firestore TTL remove expired records. Service registration stores operator-supplied names/purposes, public delivery keys, hashed credentials and approved capabilities. Temporary approval sessions last ten minutes; encrypted, unacknowledged credential deliveries last seven days. Opposite-party payloads omit emails and Firebase identities. The relay processes submitted values and an authorized agent receives explicitly shared answers. Configuration stays until deletion. When recovery backups are enabled, encrypted account/device/access metadata and webhook configuration are retained in isolated snapshots for seven days; codes, answers, request definitions and history are excluded. Independent security-denial records prevent deleted or revoked access from being restored. Lifecycle deletion is asynchronous. Infrastructure logs contain route/status metadata; application payloads are not logged. No advertising or analytics SDK is installed.

Delete your account

Use Administration → Devices → Delete account, or request deletion here. Access is blocked when accepted. Cleanup continues in the background and removes devices, connections, requests, answers, callback jobs and Firebase identity. A minimal account tombstone prevents stale access; receipt hashes expire after 24 hours and completed-job metadata after seven days. Uninstalling alone does not delete server data.
